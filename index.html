The official cooperative marketplace and mutual aid engine of Raven's Nest Temple Inc.

Core Principles

  • Accessibility First — Designed for users navigating disability, trauma, and survival conditions.
  • Radical Stability — No shifting rules, no hidden requirements, no “prove yourself” barriers.
  • Community‑Owned — Built by and for the people excluded from traditional systems.
  • Mythic Architecture — Narrative‑driven design that supports identity, grounding, and belonging.
  • Zero Exploitation — No data mining, no predatory monetization, and absolutely no surveillance.
